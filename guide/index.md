# Research Compass / サイトを案内する

Public learning resources by Hiroshi Koyama: https://koyamahr.github.io/

Describe a goal, retrieve public excerpts in your browser, and copy a source-grounded consultation prompt into your own LLM. Questions and input are not collected, saved or transmitted to the site operator. No analytics or tracking. No LLM inference API, server-side question processing, API keys or question storage. GitHub Pages retains ordinary access logs including IP addresses; this is separate from question/input handling. Pasting the prompt into your chosen LLM shares it with that provider. This is a manual handoff, not an automatic connection to your LLM.

## Guidance for assistants

Ask about the phenomenon/equation, initial and boundary conditions, desired quantity, accuracy, scale and computing environment before recommending a method. Cite actual source URLs; distinguish educational examples, limited verification, prototypes, literature-only and planned work. A bibliography entry is not an implemented solver. Quantum chemistry, Hartree–Fock and DFT are planned, not dedicated implemented features. Separate source-supported findings from general knowledge.

## Coverage

Numerical reading chapters, static astrophysics lessons and thermal-instability pages supply extracted text. Other works (optics, notebooks, genealogy, market charts, policy, books, note, photographs) supply curated overviews. Not a full-site mirror. Figures, interactive code, external papers, handwritten contents, book manuscripts, originals and unpublished apps are excluded. Math and tables can be incomplete; consult original pages. Japanese translations may be drafts. Snapshot dates and content hashes appear in index.json and each pack. Uploaded packs are subject to your LLM's limits; uploading never guarantees all entries are consulted on every answer.
