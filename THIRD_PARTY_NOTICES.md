# Third-party material and rights boundaries

- Photographs and book covers are content, excluded from the code MIT license. Book manuscripts/EPUBs are not hosted here. Listing a book does not license its contents.
- `college-notes` is a separate repository; the author's handwriting does not confer ownership of lecturers' explanations, textbook content or quoted material.
- `market-chart-atlas/data.json` contains Yahoo Finance-derived market snapshots. The MIT code license does not sublicense that data or waive provider terms. Source/retrieval metadata remains in the dataset.
- `hpci-policy/` links to MEXT, RIKEN, NDL and other public sources. Each source's conditions apply; this site does not grant permissions for linked documents, logos or institutional materials.
- `academic-roots/` records public facts and source references, not an exclusive right in facts or people's names. Visitor-entered/imported/exported lineage data is not licensed by the site operator under MIT. External AI services have their own terms.
- No bundled third-party runtime/font was identified in this static site. System fonts are used. External services (Google Forms, note, Amazon and research profiles) retain their own terms. This inventory is not a blanket clearance of every source.
